## 1. Atomic Symbols (Basic Operational Units)

Atomic symbols are indivisible basic operational units. All derived symbols and compound symbols are calculated from atomic symbols.

### 1. Heavenly Stems (Tian Gan)

- **English functional description**: The ten celestial stems used to mark years/months/days/hours, paired with five motions.
- **Detailed definition**: Jia, Yi, Bing, Ding, Wu, Ji, Geng, Xin, Ren, Gui — 10 symbols in total. Heavenly Stems pair with Five Motions, determining the Five Elements attribute and excess/deficiency of Annual Motion.
- **Operational rules**:
  - Input: Year stem (Jia/Yi/Bing/Ding/Wu/Ji/Geng/Xin/Ren/Gui)
  - Operation: Look up table (Jia-Ji transforms to Earth, Yi-Geng transforms to Metal, Bing-Xin transforms to Water, Ding-Ren transforms to Wood, Wu-Gui transforms to Fire)
  - Output: Annual Motion Five Elements + Excess/Deficiency (Yang stem = excess, Yin stem = deficiency)
- **Source**: "Suwen · Tianyuan Ji Dalun": "In Jia-Ji years, Earth Motion governs; in Yi-Geng years, Metal Motion governs; in Bing-Xin years, Water Motion governs; in Ding-Ren years, Wood Motion governs; in Wu-Gui years, Fire Motion governs."
- **Modern science correspondence**: Hypothetical correspondence — may be related to solar activity cycles and annual climate patterns, not yet verified.

### 2. Earthly Branches (Di Zhi)

- **English functional description**: The twelve earthly branches used to mark years/months/days/hours, paired with six qi.
- **Detailed definition**: Zi, Chou, Yin, Mao, Chen, Si, Wu, Wei, Shen, You, Xu, Hai — 12 symbols in total. Earthly Branches pair with Six Qi, determining Sovereign Heaven, Earthly Deputy, and Principal Qi.
- **Operational rules**:
  - Input: Year branch (Zi/Chou/Yin/Mao/Chen/Si/Wu/Wei/Shen/You/Xu/Hai)
  - Operation: Look up table (Zi-Wu Shaoyin Sovereign Fire, Chou-Wei Taiyin Damp Earth, Yin-Shen Shaoyang Ministerial Fire, Mao-You Yangming Dry Metal, Chen-Xu Taiyang Cold Water, Si-Hai Jueyin Wind Wood)
  - Output: Sovereign Heaven Six Qi + Earthly Deputy Six Qi (opposite in Yin-Yang to Sovereign Heaven)
- **Source**: "Suwen · Wuyunxing Dalun": "Above Zi-Wu, Shaoyin governs; above Chou-Wei, Taiyin governs; above Yin-Shen, Shaoyang governs; above Mao-You, Yangming governs; above Chen-Xu, Taiyang governs; above Si-Hai, Jueyin governs."
- **Modern science correspondence**: Hypothetical correspondence — may be related to Earth's orbital position and seasonal climate patterns, not yet verified.

### 3. Five Elements (Wu Xing)

- **English functional description**: Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water — five fundamental properties with generation and restriction relationships.
- **Detailed definition**: Five fundamental properties: Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water. Between the Five Elements there exist generation (Wood generates Fire, Fire generates Earth, Earth generates Metal, Metal generates Water, Water generates Wood) and restriction (Wood restricts Earth, Earth restricts Water, Water restricts Fire, Fire restricts Metal, Metal restricts Wood) relationships, which are the basis of Five Motions and Six Qi operations.
- **Operational rules**:
  - Input: Two Five Elements attributes
  - Operation: Look up generation/restriction table
  - Output: The one that generates me, the one I generate, the one that restricts me, the one I restrict
- **Source**: "Suwen · Yin Yang Yingxiang Dalun": "The East generates Wind, Wind generates Wood... The South generates Heat, Heat generates Fire... The Center generates Dampness, Dampness generates Earth... The West generates Dryness, Dryness generates Metal... The North generates Cold, Cold generates Water."
- **Modern science correspondence**: Verified correspondence — Five Elements generation-restriction relationships can be viewed as positive/negative feedback models in system dynamics, with structural similarity to homeostatic regulation mechanisms in cybernetics.

### 4. Six Qi (Liu Qi)

- **English functional description**: Wind, Cold, Summer-Heat, Dampness, Dryness, Fire — six climate types paired with three yin and three yang.
- **Detailed definition**: Six climate types: Wind, Cold, Summer-Heat, Dampness, Dryness, Fire, paired with Three Yin-Three Yang: Jueyin Wind Wood, Shaoyin Sovereign Fire, Shaoyang Ministerial Fire, Taiyin Damp Earth, Yangming Dry Metal, Taiyang Cold Water. Six Qi are divided into Principal Qi (fixed) and Guest Qi (changing), forming the basic framework of annual climate.
- **Operational rules**:
  - Input: Time period (First Qi to Final Qi)
  - Operation: Principal Qi fixed lookup; Guest Qi calculated from Sovereign Heaven
  - Output: Principal Qi + Guest Qi for that time period
- **Source**: "Suwen · Liuweizhi Dalun": "To the right of Xianming is the position of Sovereign Fire; to the right of Sovereign Fire, retreating one step, Ministerial Fire governs; advancing another step, Earth Qi governs; advancing another step, Metal Qi governs; advancing another step, Water Qi governs; advancing another step, Wood Qi governs; advancing another step, Sovereign Fire governs."
- **Modern science correspondence**: Verified correspondence — Six Qi have statistical correlation with seasonal climate patterns (temperature, humidity, wind speed), and seasonal epidemiology has confirmed the seasonal onset patterns of multiple diseases.

### 5. Three Yin-Three Yang (San Yin San Yang)

- **English functional description**: Jueyin, Shaoyin, Taiyin, Shaoyang, Yangming, Taiyang — six labels marking the quantity of yin/yang in six qi.
- **Detailed definition**: Jueyin (One Yin), Shaoyin (Two Yin), Taiyin (Three Yin), Shaoyang (One Yang), Yangming (Two Yang), Taiyang (Three Yang), used to mark the Yin-Yang attributes and quantities of Six Qi. The order of Three Yin-Three Yang determines the arrangement order of Guest Qi.
- **Operational rules**:
  - Input: Sovereign Heaven Six Qi
  - Operation: Arrange Guest Qi six steps in Three Yin-Three Yang order (Jueyin → Shaoyin → Taiyin → Shaoyang → Yangming → Taiyang)
  - Output: Guest Qi six steps (First Qi to Final Qi)
- **Source**: "Suwen · Tianyuan Ji Dalun": "Yin-Yang Qi each has quantities, hence called Three Yin-Three Yang."
- **Modern science correspondence**: Hypothetical correspondence — Three Yin-Three Yang can be viewed as classification labels for system states, with structural similarity to state space classification in cybernetics.

### 6. Twenty-Four Solar Terms (Er Shi Si Jie Qi)

- **English functional description**: 24 solar terms dividing the year into 24 periods, used to demarcate six qi time intervals.
- **Detailed definition**: Great Cold, Start of Spring, Rain Water, Awakening of Insects, Spring Equinox, Clear and Bright, Grain Rain, Start of Summer, Grain Buds, Grain in Ear, Summer Solstice, Minor Heat, Great Heat, Start of Autumn, End of Heat, White Dew, Autumn Equinox, Cold Dew, Frost's Descent, Start of Winter, Minor Snow, Major Snow, Winter Solstice, Minor Cold — 24 solar terms in total. Every 4 solar terms form one qi-step, totaling 6 qi-steps.
- **Operational rules**:
  - Input: Date
  - Operation: Look up solar term table, determine current solar term
  - Output:所属 qi-step (First Qi to Final Qi)
- **Source**: "Suwen · Liujie Zangxiang Lun": "Five days is called a pentad, three pentads is called a qi, six qi is called a season, four seasons is called a year."
- **Modern science correspondence**: Verified correspondence — Twenty-Four Solar Terms precisely correspond to solar ecliptic longitude degrees, are direct results of astronomical observations, and are consistent with seasonal divisions in modern meteorology.

### 7. Ganzhi Year Counting (Gan Zhi Ji Nian)

- **English functional description**: A 60-year cycle using stem-branch pairs to mark years, the basis of all five motions and six qi calculations.
- **Detailed definition**: Using Heavenly Stem-Earthly Branch combinations to mark years, with a 60-year cycle (Jiazi → Yichou → ... → Guihai). Ganzhi Year Counting is the basis of all Five Motions and Six Qi calculations; the year stem determines Annual Motion, and the year branch determines Sovereign Heaven.
- **Operational rules**:
  - Input: Gregorian year
  - Operation: Formula calculation (year stem = (year-3)%10, year branch = (year-3)%12)
  - Output: Year stem + Year branch
- **Source**: "Suwen · Liuweizhi Dalun": "Heavenly Qi begins with Jia, Earthly Qi begins with Zi, Zi and Jia combine, called the establishment of the year."
- **Modern science correspondence**: Verified correspondence — Ganzhi Year Counting is a direct result of astronomical calendars, with structural similarity to the least common multiple (approximately 60 years) of Jupiter's orbital period (approximately 11.86 years) and Saturn's orbital period (approximately 29.46 years).

### 8. Five Tones (Wu Yin)

- **English functional description**: Jue, Zhi, Gong, Shang, Yu — five musical tones used to mark the excess/deficiency of host and guest motions.
- **Detailed definition**: Five musical scales: Jue, Zhi, Gong, Shang, Yu, used to mark the "greater/lesser" (excess/deficiency) of Principal Motion and Guest Motion. Greater Jue = Wood excess, Lesser Jue = Wood deficiency, and so on.
- **Operational rules**:
  - Input: Annual Motion Five Elements + Excess/Deficiency
  - Operation: Look up table (Greater Jue/Lesser Jue → Wood, Greater Zhi/Lesser Zhi → Fire...)
  - Output: Greater/Lesser arrangement of Principal Motion/Guest Motion five steps
- **Source**: "Suwen · Liuyuan Zhengji Dalun": "First establish the year to clarify its qi, the numbers of Metal-Wood-Water-Fire-Earth motion, the transformations of Cold-Heat-Damp-Dry-Wind-Fire governance, then the Way of Heaven can be seen, and people's qi can be regulated."
- **Modern science correspondence**: Hypothetical correspondence — Five Tones have structural similarity to frequency resonance, may be related to frequency coupling of biological rhythms, not yet verified.

### 9. Normalization (Zheng Hua)

- **English functional description**: The direct correspondence between a branch and its qi, with coefficient 1.0 (e.g., Wu → Shaoyin Sovereign Fire).
- **Detailed definition**: The forward correspondence relationship between Earthly Branch and Six Qi, with coefficient 1.0. E.g., Wu → Shaoyin Sovereign Fire (Normalization), Wei → Taiyin Damp Earth (Normalization), Shen → Shaoyang Ministerial Fire (Normalization), You → Yangming Dry Metal (Normalization), Xu → Taiyang Cold Water (Normalization), Hai → Jueyin Wind Wood (Normalization).
- **Operational rules**:
  - Input: Earthly Branch
  - Operation: Look up Normalization/Opposition table
  - Output: Six Qi + Coefficient (Normalization = 1.0)
- **Source**: "Suwen · Liuweizhi Dalun": "Normalization is generated from the root."
- **Modern science correspondence**: Hypothetical correspondence — Normalization/Opposition can be viewed as weighting coefficients for signal strength, with structural similarity to gain control in signal processing.

### 10. Opposition (Dui Hua)

- **English functional description**: The opposite correspondence between a branch and its qi, with coefficient 0.7 (e.g., Zi → Shaoyin Sovereign Fire).
- **Detailed definition**: The opposite correspondence relationship between Earthly Branch and Six Qi, with coefficient 0.7. E.g., Zi → Shaoyin Sovereign Fire (Opposition), Chou → Taiyin Damp Earth (Opposition), Yin → Shaoyang Ministerial Fire (Opposition), Mao → Yangming Dry Metal (Opposition), Chen → Taiyang Cold Water (Opposition), Si → Jueyin Wind Wood (Opposition). The qi intensity of Opposition is lower than Normalization.
- **Operational rules**:
  - Input: Earthly Branch
  - Operation: Look up Normalization/Opposition table
  - Output: Six Qi + Coefficient (Opposition = 0.7)
- **Source**: "Suwen · Liuweizhi Dalun": "Opposition is generated from the branch."
- **Modern science correspondence**: Hypothetical correspondence — Opposition coefficient 0.7 can be viewed as a signal attenuation factor, with structural similarity to the distance attenuation law in physics.

## 2. Derived Symbols (Calculated from Atomic Symbols)

Derived symbols are calculated from atomic symbols through fixed rules, and are the core operational results of Five Motions and Six Qi.

### 11. Annual Motion (Sui Yun)

- **Aliases**: Central Motion, Great Motion
- **English functional description**: The five-element motion governing the whole year, determined by the year stem, divided into excess and deficiency.
- **Detailed definition**: The Five Motions qi governing the entire year, determined by the year stem. Annual Motion is divided into Excess (Yang stem) and Deficiency (Yin stem), is the overall framework of annual climate change, and is also the starting point of victory-restoration and stagnation-eruption.
- **Operational rules**:
  - Input: Year stem
  - Operation: Look up table (Jia-Ji Earth, Yi-Geng Metal, Bing-Xin Water, Ding-Ren Wood, Wu-Gui Fire) + Determine Excess/Deficiency (Yang stem = Excess, Yin stem = Deficiency)
  - Output: Annual Motion Five Elements + Excess/Deficiency + Power value (Excess = +6~+10, Deficiency = -6~-10)
- **Source**: "Suwen · Tianyuan Ji Dalun": "In Jia-Ji years, Earth Motion governs..."
- **Modern science correspondence**: Hypothetical correspondence — Annual Motion may be related to annual climate anomaly patterns (e.g., El Niño/La Niña cycles), not yet verified.

### 12. Host Motion (Zhu Yun)

- **English functional description**: The fixed five-step motion of each year (Wood→Fire→Earth→Metal→Water), with excess/deficiency marked by five tones.
- **Detailed definition**: The fixed five-step motion of each year, arranged in Wood→Fire→Earth→Metal→Water order, each step governing approximately 73 days. The greater/lesser of Host Motion is determined by Annual Motion; if Annual Motion is excess, Host Motion is greater; if Annual Motion is deficient, Host Motion is lesser.
- **Operational rules**:
  - Input: Annual Motion Five Elements + Excess/Deficiency
  - Operation: Use Annual Motion as initial motion, arrange five steps by greater-lesser generation
  - Output: Five Elements + Greater/Lesser of Host Motion five steps (Initial Motion to Final Motion)
- **Source**: "Suwen · Liuyuan Zhengji Dalun": "In Wood Motion excess years, Wind Qi prevails... In Wood Motion deficiency years, Dryness prevails..."
- **Modern science correspondence**: Hypothetical correspondence — Host Motion is related to seasonal climate gradual change patterns, consistent with seasonal change laws in modern phenology.

### 13. Guest Motion (Ke Yun)

- **English functional description**: The changing five-step motion of each year, starting from the annual motion as the initial step, arranged by excess/deficiency generation.
- **Detailed definition**: The changing five-step motion of each year, using Annual Motion as the initial step, arranged in greater-lesser generation order. Guest Motion superimposes with Host Motion, jointly affecting climate change in each time period of the year.
- **Operational rules**:
  - Input: Annual Motion Five Elements + Excess/Deficiency
  - Operation: Use Annual Motion as initial motion, arrange five steps by greater-lesser generation (same as Host Motion, but different starting point)
  - Output: Five Elements + Greater/Lesser of Guest Motion five steps (Initial Motion to Final Motion)
- **Source**: "Suwen · Liuyuan Zhengji Dalun": "Guest Motion is superimposed on Host Motion, changing each year."
- **Modern science correspondence**: Hypothetical correspondence — Guest Motion is related to inter-annual climate difference patterns, with structural similarity to inter-annual variability in modern climatology.

### 14. Host Qi (Zhu Qi)

- **English functional description**: The fixed six-step qi of each year (Jueyin Wind-Wood → Shaoyin Sovereign-Fire → Shaoyang Minister-Fire → Taiyin Damp-Earth → Yangming Dry-Metal → Taiyang Cold-Water).
- **Detailed definition**: The fixed six-step qi of each year, arranged in Jueyin Wind Wood → Shaoyin Sovereign Fire → Shaoyang Ministerial Fire → Taiyin Damp Earth → Yangming Dry Metal → Taiyang Cold Water order, each step governing approximately 60 days. Host Qi is the fixed framework of annual climate change.
- **Operational rules**:
  - Input: Time period (First Qi to Final Qi)
  - Operation: Fixed lookup
  - Output: Host Qi Six Qi for that time period
- **Source**: "Suwen · Liuweizhi Dalun": "I wish to hear how the geography corresponds to the six qi seasonal positions? Qi Bo said: To the right of Xianming is the position of Sovereign Fire..."
- **Modern science correspondence**: Verified correspondence — Host Qi is highly consistent with seasonal climate (spring warmth, summer heat, late summer dampness, autumn dryness, winter cold), and is a direct reflection of astronomical seasons.

### 15. Guest Qi (Ke Qi)

- **English functional description**: The changing six-step qi of each year, with the 3rd step determined by sovereign heaven, arranged in three-yin-three-yang order.
- **Detailed definition**: The changing six-step qi of each year, with Sovereign Heaven determining the Third Qi, then arranging the remaining five steps in Three Yin-Three Yang order (Jueyin → Shaoyin → Taiyin → Shaoyang → Yangming → Taiyang). Guest Qi superimposes with Host Qi (Guest-Principal Superimposition), jointly affecting climate change in each time period of the year.
- **Operational rules**:
  - Input: Year branch (→ Sovereign Heaven)
  - Operation: Use Sovereign Heaven as Third Qi, arrange six steps in Three Yin-Three Yang order
  - Output: Six Qi of Guest Qi six steps (First Qi to Final Qi)
- **Source**: "Suwen · Liuweizhi Dalun": "Above and below have positions, left and right have order. Hence to the right of Shaoyang, Yangming governs; to the right of Yangming, Taiyang governs..."
- **Modern science correspondence**: Hypothetical correspondence — Guest Qi is related to inter-annual climate anomaly patterns, with structural similarity to inter-annual variability of atmospheric circulation in modern climatology.

### 16. Sovereign Heaven (Si Tian)

- **English functional description**: The 3rd guest qi-step governing the first half of the year, determined by the year branch.
- **Detailed definition**: Guest Qi Third Qi, governing climate change in the first half of the year (First Qi to Third Qi), determined by the year branch. Sovereign Heaven is the "overall framework" of annual Guest Qi, determining the arrangement of Guest Qi six steps.

### 17. Earthly Deputy (Zai Quan)

- **English functional description**: The 6th guest qi-step governing the second half of the year, opposite in yin-yang to sovereign heaven.
- **Detailed definition**: Guest Qi Final Qi, governing climate change in the second half of the year (Fourth Qi to Final Qi), opposite in Yin-Yang to Sovereign Heaven (e.g., Sovereign Heaven Shaoyin Sovereign Fire → Earthly Deputy Yangming Dry Metal). Earthly Deputy and Sovereign Heaven jointly determine the framework of annual Guest Qi.

### 18. Interval Qi (Jian Qi)

- **English functional description**: The four guest qi-steps excluding sovereign heaven (3rd) and earthly spring (6th), including left and right interval qi.
- **Detailed definition**: The four qi-steps in Guest Qi excluding Sovereign Heaven (Third Qi) and Earthly Deputy (Final Qi), including Sovereign Heaven Left Interval (Fourth Qi), Sovereign Heaven Right Interval (Second Qi), Earthly Deputy Left Interval (First Qi), Earthly Deputy Right Interval (Fifth Qi). Interval Qi governs climate change in each time period.

### 19. Normal Qi (Ping Qi)

- **English functional description**: A year where annual motion is neither excess nor deficiency, formed when excess is suppressed or deficiency is assisted.
- **Detailed definition**: A special year where Annual Motion is neither excess nor deficiency. Normal Qi forms in two situations: ① Annual Motion excess but suppressed by Sovereign Heaven/Earthly Deputy (e.g., Wuchen year, Annual Motion Fire excess, Sovereign Heaven Taiyang Cold Water suppresses); ② Annual Motion deficiency but assisted by Sovereign Heaven/Earthly Deputy/year branch (e.g., Yimao year, Annual Motion Metal deficiency, year branch Mao Metal assists). In Normal Qi years, climate change is relatively mild.

## 3. Compound Symbols (Derived from Combinations of Derived Symbols)

Compound symbols are derived from two or more derived symbols through fixed rules, describing complex relationships between Five Motions and Six Qi.

### 20. Guest-Host Interaction (Ke Zhu Jia Lin)

- **English functional description**: The superposition of guest qi and host qi in the same time period, divided into harmonious (相得) and disharmonious (不相得).
- **Detailed definition**: The superimposition relationship between Guest Qi and Host Qi in the same time period (First Qi to Final Qi). Guest-Host Interaction is divided into "harmonious" (Guest Qi generates Host Qi, or Guest and Host are same qi) and "disharmonious" (Guest Qi restricts Host Qi, or Host Qi restricts Guest Qi). Harmonious means mild climate, disharmonious means climate anomaly and prone to disease.
- **Operational rules**:
  - Input: Guest Qi + Host Qi for a certain time period
  - Operation: Look up Five Elements generation-restriction table, determine relationship between Guest Qi and Host Qi
  - Output: Harmonious/Disharmonious + Specific relationship (generation/restriction/same qi)
- **Source**: "Suwen · Liuweizhi Dalun": "When qi is harmonious, there is peace; when not harmonious, there is disease."
- **Modern science correspondence**: Hypothetical correspondence — Guest-Host Interaction can be viewed as the superposition effect of two climate patterns, with structural similarity to weather system superposition in meteorology.

### 21. Heavenly Concordance (Tian Fu)

- **English functional description**: A special year where the annual motion element equals the sovereign heaven element (e.g., Wu Wu year: Fire motion + Shaoyin Sovereign Fire).
- **Detailed definition**: A special year where Annual Motion Five Elements equals Sovereign Heaven Five Elements. E.g., Wuwu year, Annual Motion Fire (Wu), Sovereign Heaven Shaoyin Sovereign Fire (Wu), Fire and Fire are the same, hence Heavenly Concordance. In Heavenly Concordance years, climate change is severe, "its disease is rapid and dangerous."
- **Operational rules**:

  - Operation: Compare whether the two are the same
  - Output: Yes/No Heavenly Concordance
- **Source**: "Suwen · Liuweizhi Dalun": "Heavenly Concordance is when Annual Motion and Sovereign Heaven are the same qi."
- **Modern science correspondence**: Hypothetical correspondence — Heavenly Concordance may be related to resonance years of climate anomalies, not yet verified.
