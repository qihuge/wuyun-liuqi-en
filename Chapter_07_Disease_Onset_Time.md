# Chapter 7: Principal-Guest Motion and Qi Calculation at Disease Onset Time

## 1. Why Principal-Guest Motion and Qi Calculation at Disease Onset Time is Needed

### 7.1 Interaction Between Congenital Endowment and Acquired Time

In Chapter 6, we calculated Dynamic Stagnation Points based on birth time (congenital endowment) — Trigger Points, Stagnation Points, Core Organs, Affected Meridians, High-Risk Periods, and Exacerbation Periods. These parameters describe an individual's "innate" constitutional weaknesses and disease tendencies.

However, disease onset depends not only on congenital endowment but also on the Five Motions and Six Qi configuration at **acquired time** — how the Five Motions and Six Qi of the current year, current month, and current date interact with congenital endowment, thereby triggering or exacerbating disease.

Analogy: Congenital endowment determines "where the explosive barrels are" (Core Organs, Stagnation Points), while acquired time determines "when there will be sparks" (trigger factors). Only when both "explosive barrels" and "sparks" exist simultaneously will disease onset occur.

Therefore, to accurately predict the onset time and severity of disease, it is necessary to perform **Principal-Guest Motion and Qi Calculation at Disease Onset Time** — combining congenital endowment with the Five Motions and Six Qi configuration at the current time (or disease onset time) to dynamically calculate disease onset risk.

### 7.2 Meaning of Principal-Guest Motion and Qi

In this chapter, "Principal-Guest Motion and Qi" refers to:

- **Principal Motion/Principal Qi**: Fixed, unchanging Five Motions and Six Qi configuration every year (five steps of Principal Motion, six steps of Principal Qi)
- **Guest Motion/Guest Qi**: Changing, different Five Motions and Six Qi configuration every year (five steps of Guest Motion, six steps of Guest Qi)
- **Principal-Guest Superimposition**: Superimposition of Principal Motion and Guest Motion, superimposition of Principal Qi and Guest Qi (Guest-Principal Superimposition)

Principal-Guest Motion and Qi Calculation at Disease Onset Time is to calculate the Principal Motion, Guest Motion, Principal Qi, and Guest Qi at the disease onset time (year, month, date), as well as their interaction with congenital endowment.

The calculation of Day Stem-Branch is relatively complex, usually requiring checking a perpetual calendar, or calculating with a formula. The commonly used formula is:

```
Day Stem-Branch serial number = (known reference day's stem-branch serial number + number of days difference) % 60
```

For example, knowing that January 1, 1900 was the Jia Xu day (stem-branch serial number = 10), the Day Stem-Branch of any date can be calculated by calculating the number of days difference from January 1, 1900.

## 3. Interaction Between Congenital Endowment and Current Time

### 7.7 Core Logic of Interaction

The core logic of the interaction between congenital endowment and current time is **"like attracts like, different restricts each other"**:

- **Like attracts like**: If the Five Elements/Six Qi of the current time are the same as those of congenital endowment, they will "superimpose" and "amplify" — congenital weaknesses are amplified, and disease is prone to onset or exacerbation.
- **Different restricts each other**: If the Five Elements/Six Qi of the current time restrict those of congenital endowment, they will "constrain" and "balance" — congenital weaknesses are constrained, and disease may be relieved or not onset.

Specifically, the interaction is reflected in the following levels:

### 7.8 Interaction at Annual Motion Level

**Relationship between current Annual Motion and congenital Annual Motion**:

| Relationship | Meaning | Impact on Disease |
|-------------|---------|------------------|
| Same Qi (same Five Elements) | Current Annual Motion and congenital Annual Motion have the same Five Elements | Congenital excess/deficiency is amplified, disease prone to onset or exacerbation |
| Generation (current generates congenital) | Current Annual Motion Five Elements generates congenital Annual Motion Five Elements | Congenital deficiency is supplemented, disease may be relieved |
| Generation (congenital generates current) | Congenital Annual Motion Five Elements generates current Annual Motion Five Elements | Congenital energy is consumed, disease may be exacerbated |
| Restriction (current restricts congenital) | Current Annual Motion Five Elements restricts congenital Annual Motion Five Elements | Congenital excess is constrained, disease may be relieved |
| Restriction (congenital restricts current) | Congenital Annual Motion Five Elements restricts current Annual Motion Five Elements | Congenital energy is consumed, disease may be exacerbated |

**Example**:
- Congenital Annual Motion = Metal Excess (Lung excessive, Liver restricted), current Annual Motion = Metal Excess → Like attracts like, Metal qi more excessive, Liver restricted more severely, Liver disease prone to exacerbation.
- Congenital Annual Motion = Metal Excess, current Annual Motion = Fire Excess → Fire restricts Metal, current Annual Motion constrains congenital Annual Motion excess, Metal qi balanced, Lung disease may be relieved.
- Congenital Annual Motion = Metal Excess, current Annual Motion = Water Excess → Metal generates Water, congenital Annual Motion generates current Annual Motion, Metal qi consumed, Lung excess relieved, but Kidney qi may be excessive.

### 7.9 Interaction at Sovereign Heaven-Earthly Deputy Level

**Relationship between current Sovereign Heaven/Earthly Deputy and congenital Sovereign Heaven/Earthly Deputy**:

Similar to the Annual Motion level, the Five Elements relationship between current Sovereign Heaven/Earthly Deputy and congenital Sovereign Heaven/Earthly Deputy also affects disease onset or relief.

Special attention should be paid to:
- If current Sovereign Heaven has the same Five Elements as congenital Stagnation Point, then "like attracts like," Stagnation Point amplified, disease prone to onset in the first half of the year.
- If current Earthly Deputy has the same Five Elements as congenital Stagnation Point, then "like attracts like," Stagnation Point amplified, disease prone to onset in the second half of the year.
- If current Sovereign Heaven/Earthly Deputy Five Elements restrict congenital Stagnation Point Five Elements, then "different restricts each other," Stagnation Point constrained, disease may be relieved.

### 7.10 Interaction at Principal Qi-Guest Qi Level

**Relationship between current Principal Qi/Guest Qi and congenital Trigger Point/Stagnation Point**:

The relationship between the Principal Qi and Guest Qi of the current month and the Six Qi attributes of congenital Trigger Point and Stagnation Point directly affects the disease onset risk in that month:

- If current Principal Qi/Guest Qi is the same as congenital Trigger Point, Trigger Point activated, disease prone to exacerbation.
- If current Principal Qi/Guest Qi is the same as congenital Stagnation Point, Stagnation Point activated, disease prone to onset.
- If current Principal Qi/Guest Qi restricts congenital Trigger Point/Stagnation Point, Trigger Point/Stagnation Point constrained, disease may be relieved.
- If current Principal Qi/Guest Qi generates congenital Trigger Point/Stagnation Point, Trigger Point/Stagnation Point promoted, disease may be exacerbated.

## 5. Calculation of Trigger Point and Stagnation Point at Disease Onset Time

### 7.14 Calculation of Trigger Point at Disease Onset Time

In Chapter 6, we calculated the Trigger Point and Stagnation Point based on birth time (congenital endowment). Now, we need to calculate the Trigger Point and Stagnation Point based on disease onset time (current time), and then combine the two to comprehensively judge disease onset risk.

**Calculation Method of Trigger Point at Disease Onset Time**:
```
Input: Annual Motion of disease onset year + Four Pillars at disease onset time (Year Branch, Month Branch, Day Branch, Hour Branch)

Step 1: Determine the Annual Motion Five Elements and Excess/Deficiency of the disease onset year
Step 2: According to the Trigger Point calculation method in Chapter 6 (for Excess years find "what I restrict", for Deficiency years find "what restricts me"), search for the Trigger Point in the Four Pillars at disease onset time
  - Note: Adopt the "Six Qi as Principal, Four Directions as Auxiliary" strategy — prioritize searching with Six Qi Five Elements, then supplement with Four Directions Five Elements when not found

Output: Disease onset time Trigger Point + source (Six Qi/Four Directions)
```

**Note**:
- The Trigger Point at disease onset time may be different from the congenital Trigger Point. The Trigger Point at disease onset time describes the "attacker at the current time," while the congenital Trigger Point describes the "attacker at birth." Combining both can more accurately judge the disease onset mechanism.
- The calculation of Trigger Points adopts the "Six Qi as Principal, Four Directions as Auxiliary" strategy (see Chapter 6 for details), and Core Organs adopt the "principal+auxiliary" structure (Six Qi organs as principal, Four Directions organs as auxiliary).

## 6. Time Window Prediction of Disease Onset

### 7.17 Calculation of Onset Risk Index

Combining all the above factors, we can calculate an "Onset Risk Index" to quantify the disease onset risk in a certain time period.

**Calculation Formula of Onset Risk Index**:
```
Onset Risk Index = 
  Annual Motion interaction weight × Annual Motion interaction score +
  Sovereign Heaven-Earthly Deputy interaction weight × Sovereign Heaven-Earthly Deputy interaction score +
  Principal-Guest Qi interaction weight × Principal-Guest Qi interaction score +
  Trigger Point-Stagnation Point matching weight × Trigger Point-Stagnation Point matching score +
  Six Qi brake weight × Six Qi brake score
```

**Scoring Rules for Each Factor**:

| Factor | High Risk (+2 points) | Medium Risk (+1 point) | Low Risk (0 points) | Protection (-1 point) |
|--------|----------------------|----------------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Annual Motion interaction | Same Qi (superimposition amplification) | Congenital generates current (consumption) | Generation (current generates congenital, supplementation) | Restriction (current restricts congenital, constraint) |
| Sovereign Heaven-Earthly Deputy interaction | Sovereign Heaven/Earthly Deputy = congenital Stagnation Point (same Qi) | Sovereign Heaven/Earthly Deputy generates congenital Stagnation Point (promotion) | Irrelevant | Sovereign Heaven/Earthly Deputy restricts congenital Stagnation Point (constraint) |
| Principal-Guest Qi interaction | Guest Qi = congenital Stagnation Point (same Qi) | Guest Qi generates congenital Stagnation Point (promotion) | Irrelevant | Guest Qi restricts congenital Stagnation Point (constraint) |
| Trigger Point-Stagnation Point matching | Disease onset Trigger Point = congenital Stagnation Point (direct attack) | Disease onset Trigger Point = congenital Trigger Point (old enemy reunion) | Irrelevant | Disease onset Stagnation Point = congenital Trigger Point (counterattack) |
| Six Qi brake | Brake value negative (promotes attack) | Brake value low (weak braking) | Brake value medium | Brake value high (strong braking) |

**Weight Settings** (default values, to be verified):
- Annual Motion interaction weight = 0.2
- Sovereign Heaven-Earthly Deputy interaction weight = 0.2
- Principal-Guest Qi interaction weight = 0.2
- Trigger Point-Stagnation Point matching weight = 0.3 (most important)
- Six Qi brake weight = 0.1

**Value Range of Onset Risk Index**: -2 (lowest risk) to +2 (highest risk).

**Risk Level Classification**:
- > 1.0: Extremely high risk, disease very likely to onset or exacerbate
- 0.5 - 1.0: High risk, disease may onset or exacerbate
- 0 - 0.5: Medium risk, disease has possibility of onset
- -0.5 - 0: Low risk, disease unlikely to onset
- < -0.5: Extremely low risk, disease may be relieved

### 7.18 Prediction Method of Time Window

With the Onset Risk Index, we can predict the time window of disease onset:

**Step 1**: Calculate the Onset Risk Index for each month in the next 12 months (or 24 months).
**Step 2**: Find months with Onset Risk Index > 0.5, mark as "high-risk months."
**Step 3**: Among high-risk months, find months with Onset Risk Index > 1.0, mark as "extremely high-risk months."
**Step 4**: Among extremely high-risk months, further calculate the Onset Risk Index for each solar term (or each 10-day period), find the specific time period with the highest risk.
**Step 5**: Output the time window prediction of disease onset (high-risk months, extremely high-risk months, specific high-risk time periods).

This time window prediction can provide precise time guidance for disease prevention and intervention — before the arrival of high-risk time periods, take preventive measures in advance to avoid disease onset or exacerbation.

## 8. Distinguishing Initial Onset from Recurrence, Exacerbation from Remission

### 7.20 Distinguishing Initial Onset from Recurrence

Through the matching relationship between congenital Trigger Point/Stagnation Point and disease onset time Trigger Point/Stagnation Point, we can distinguish "initial onset" and "recurrence" of disease:

**Initial Onset** (first onset):
- Disease onset time Trigger Point ≠ congenital Trigger Point, and disease onset time Stagnation Point ≠ congenital Stagnation Point
- Meaning: New attacker and new lesion appear, disease is first onset
- Characteristics: Onset mechanism has little relationship with congenital endowment, more related to environmental factors at the current time

**Recurrence** (re-onset):
- Disease onset time Trigger Point = congenital Trigger Point, or disease onset time Stagnation Point = congenital Stagnation Point
- Meaning: Old attacker or old lesion reappears, disease recurs
- Characteristics: Onset mechanism is closely related to congenital endowment, is "old wound recurrence"

**Severity of Recurrence**:
- If disease onset time Trigger Point = congenital Stagnation Point (attacker is exactly congenital lesion), recurrence is most severe
- If disease onset time Trigger Point = congenital Trigger Point (old enemy reunion), recurrence is relatively severe
- If disease onset time Stagnation Point = congenital Stagnation Point (old lesion attacked again), recurrence is moderately severe

### 7.21 Distinguishing Exacerbation from Remission

Through the changing trend of the Onset Risk Index, we can distinguish "exacerbation" and "remission" of disease:

**Exacerbation**:
- Onset Risk Index increases month by month
- Or Onset Risk Index > 0.5 (high risk)
- Meaning: Disease is exacerbating, or about to exacerbate
- Intervention suggestion: Strengthen monitoring, intervene in advance, avoid inducing factors

**Remission**:
- Onset Risk Index decreases month by month
- Or Onset Risk Index < 0 (low risk/protection)
- Meaning: Disease is remitting, or in stable period
- Intervention suggestion: Consolidate treatment, regulate constitution, prevent recurrence

**Stable**:
- Onset Risk Index fluctuates around 0 (-0.5 to 0.5)
- Meaning: Disease is in stable state, neither obviously exacerbating nor obviously remitting
- Intervention suggestion: Maintain treatment, regular monitoring

## 9. Falsifiability of Principal-Guest Motion and Qi Calculation at Disease Onset Time

### 7.22 Falsifiable Predictions

Principal-Guest Motion and Qi Calculation at Disease Onset Time is falsifiable because it can make clear, testable predictions:

**Prediction 1: Onset Time Prediction**
- Prediction content: The high-risk months calculated based on the Onset Risk Index should be consistent with the patient's actual disease onset time.
- Test method: Longitudinal cohort study, tracking and observing patients' disease onset times, comparing with calculated high-risk months.
- Falsification condition: If the proportion of disease onset times within the calculated high-risk months is not significantly higher than random level, then Prediction 1 is falsified.
