**Explanation of Two Schools of Earthly Branch Five Elements**:

### G.2 High-Risk Period / Exacerbation Period Calculation Rules

**High-Risk Period**:
- Based on the Four Directions Five Elements of the Stagnation Point, find all Earthly Branches of the same Five Elements.
- The months corresponding to these Earthly Branches are the High-Risk Period.
- Example: Stagnation Point = Wu, Four Directions Five Elements = Fire → High-Risk Period = Si month + Wu month (Summer, Fire exuberant).

**Exacerbation Period**:
- Based on the Four Directions Five Elements of the Trigger Point, find all Earthly Branches of the same Five Elements.
- The months corresponding to these Earthly Branches are the Exacerbation Period.
- Example: Trigger Point = Hai, Four Directions Five Elements = Water → Exacerbation Period = Hai month + Zi month (Winter, Water exuberant).

**Note**:
- The Five Elements grouping for High-Risk Periods and Exacerbation Periods is based on Four Directions Five Elements (direction/season Five Elements), because the Five Elements attributes of months/seasons are mainly determined by direction/season, not Six Qi Five Elements.
- Earth exuberant includes four months (Chen-Xu-Chou-Wei), with a relatively large range, requiring special attention.
- High-Risk Periods and Exacerbation Periods may overlap (e.g., when the Four Directions Five Elements of the Stagnation Point and Trigger Point are the same), at which point disease risk is highest.
